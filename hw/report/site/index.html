ESP32-S31 SDR: two-board design report

Receive-only SDR: wideband front end, RFFC5072 mixer, 2.44 GHz IF into the ESP32-S31 raw I/Q stream over gigabit Ethernet. Status 2026-11-10: both boards placed, routed and DRC-clean to JLC basic 4-layer rules, with fab outputs generated. Not ordered, because of stock.

Architecture

Board B (RF): SMA ─ ESD ─ SP3T ─┬─ LB: PSA4-5043+ ─ LPF 1.75 GHz ─┐ ├─ MB: PSA4-5043+ ─ BPF 1.75–2.15 ─┤─ SP3T ─ GVA-62+ ─ balun ─ RFFC5072 ─ 2.44 GHz SAW ─ 3 dB pad ─ U.FL └─ HB: HPF ─ QPL9503 ─ HPF 3.1 GHz ┘ (40 MHz TCXO ref; LO 290 MHz–4.2 GHz) Board A (base): U.FL ─ ESP32-S31-WROOM-1U (raw I/Q, 40 MS/s) ─ RGMII ─ YT8531 ─ RJ45 (GbE); USB-C power; 2×10 header → Board B control + 5 V

Tuning: LB 50–1750 MHz (LO = IF+RF), MB 1750–2150 MHz (LO = IF−RF), HB 3.1–6 GHz (LO = RF−IF), plus native ESP32 2.15–2.8 GHz on Board A alone (antenna on the module's U.FL). There is a gap at 2.8–3.1 GHz. The IF is 61 × 40 MHz, which parks the reference harmonic on the DC spike.

Known RF limitations (from the frequency-plan analysis):
  • In the HB, 1×3 / 1×5 mixer spurs fall in-band at 15–22 dBc for tuning around 3.5–4.1 GHz and 4.5–5.26 GHz. Software should flag those ranges.
  • The MB BPF costs up to 4.1 dB, so the MB is the noisiest mixer band.
  • Rates below 40 MS/s decimate without filtering in the current esp-sdr firmware (aliasing).

Layout and process

  • Process (D22): JLC basic 4-layer, JLC04161H-3313 stack, no surcharge items. 0.45/0.2 mm vias, 0.1/0.1 mm track/space, 0.2 mm hole and edge clearance. Verified on the final boards: 0 tracks on In1, no via outside the no-surcharge range.
  • RF lines: 0.19 mm microstrip over In1 GND (0.1 mm prepreg) = 50.8 Ω (Sonnet), ≈ ±10 % with JLC tolerance. The RF copper is locked exactly as in the EM model.
  • DRC (KiCad, JLC rules): 0 unconnected / shorts / clearance / hole-clearance on both boards. Accepted flags:
    • edge-launch SMA copper at the board edge;
    • courtyard overlaps in the RF ladders at the EM-model spacing;
    • silk.
  • Board A:
    • RGMII module-to-PHY lanes are scripted: intra-group skew ≈ 50 ps, far inside RGMII-ID tolerance.
    • TX 33 Ω resistors were dropped (D21): the reference places them at the MAC, which is impossible under the module.
    • PHY decoupling sits at the pins.
  • Board B:
    • The RFFC5072 loop filter is pinned next to the mixer; all LF nets are ≤12 mm on F.Cu.
    • Three scripted hand fixes.
  • Autorouter: Freerouting, statically audited (not malicious) and run network-isolated in a memory-capped unit.

Before ordering

  1. Board A USB HS pair: 13.5 mm intra-pair skew (≈90 ps) and 4 vias. Re-route as a coupled 90 Ω pair, or accept full-speed USB (Ethernet is the data path).
  2. Board B 40 MHz reference stub: 43 mm on REFCLK_RF because the DNP option resistors R704/R708 sit at the south edge. Move them next to the TCXO.
  3. JLC CPL/DFM preview: check part rotations, and that JLC accepts the 0402 RF ladders at the EM-model pad gaps.
  4. Sourcing: WROOM-1U is not on LCSC; RFFC5072 has 66 in stock and the T2 IF balun 25 (as of 2026-11-10). Re-check stock first.
  5. Get a live JLC quote with these Gerbers/BOM/CPL to replace the PCB and shipping assumptions.
